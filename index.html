Meet your coach

Meet Nathan.

A PGA of Canada Class A Professional, I believe great coaching is about more than building a better golf swing. I’m passionate about helping golfers of all ages and abilities discover their own unique love for the game, build confidence, and enjoy the process of improving.

Junior golf and player development are especially close to my heart. Whether someone is picking up a club for the first time or preparing for competition, I work to make the complicated parts of golf simple, relatable, and achievable. I want every player to understand that there’s so much more to this game than the final score.

I’ve worked in golf and coaching since 2016 and became a PGA of Canada Professional in 2020. I’m an Associate Professional at Highlands Golf Club in Edmonton and Head Coach of the Northwestern Polytechnic Wolves golf program. My experience also includes coaching at NAIT, serving as an Alberta Summer Games Zone Coach, and running a junior golf academy. I’ve been fortunate to help players reach provincial championships and national collegiate competitions, and am proud to have seen over 10 junior students continue onto the collegiate level.
